# Home page blocks

Each block starts with placeholder text and images; replace them with your own. Untouched Arabic default text shows
in English on English pages. Product and image fields start empty, so any block can be saved without filling them in.

## Zahiya's blocks

| Block | Use it for | Main settings |
|---|---|---|
| Zahiya: hero slider | A full-screen photo or video with a large title | Slides (image, phone image, MP4/WebM video, texts, button), autoplay, interval, height, text alignment, text colour |
| Zahiya: text section | A centred paragraph | Eyebrow, title, description, button |
| Zahiya: promo banners | 1 to 4 banners with titles over the photo | Image, title, subtitle, link |
| Zahiya: product showcase | Products in a carousel or grid | Source (latest, category, brand, tag, or products you pick), count, layout, columns, "View all" button |
| Zahiya: image with text | Photo beside text, text over a photo, or a colour panel | Layout, image side, panel colour |
| Zahiya: FAQ | Questions that open on click, with a photo | Questions and answers, show photo |
| Zahiya: review spotlight | One large review at a time | Rating, title, text, name, role, photo |
| Zahiya: deal product with countdown | A discounted product with a countdown | Product, end date (text such as `2026-12-31 23:59`; when empty, the product's own discount end is used), photo, description |
| Zahiya: scrolling text | Words moving across the page | Texts (optional image), speed, size, colours |
| Zahiya: before and after | Compare two photos by dragging | Both photos and their labels |
| Zahiya: shoppable lookbook | A photo with points that open products | Photo, points (product, horizontal and vertical position in percent) |
| Zahiya: shoppable reels | Photos or videos, each with a product | Posts (photo, video, product), two follow buttons |
| Zahiya: store features | 1 to 4 features with icons | Icon, title, text, link |
| Zahiya: category collage | Photo tiles leading to categories | Photos and links |
| Zahiya: brands grid | Brand logos | Brands |
| Zahiya: blog posts | Latest posts or posts you pick | Posts, count, "View all" button |
| Zahiya: customer reviews | Several reviews in a carousel | Reviews |
| Zahiya: social gallery | Photos from your social accounts | Photos, profile link |
| Zahiya: join us band | A sign-up invitation band | Texts, button, link, background colour |

Salla doesn't offer newsletter sign-up to themes, so "Join us" is a button, not an email field: it follows your link,
or opens the sign-up window for guests.

## Base theme blocks

The base theme's 11 blocks (animated images, quick links, featured products, brands, blog and others) are still
available.

## A suggested home page

Roughly in this order: hero slider, text section, promo banners, product showcase, image with text, FAQ, review
spotlight, deal product, scrolling text, before and after, lookbook, shoppable reels, store features. You can add the
same block more than once, for example a second hero near the bottom.

## Tips

- Use photos with similar proportions within one block, and a separate phone image in the hero slider when needed.
- Check your pages in dark mode if you keep the mode switch visible.
