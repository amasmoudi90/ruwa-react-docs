# Getting started

Ruwa is a theme for beauty and skincare stores. It works in Arabic and English, in light and dark mode, on phones
and computers.

## Where to find the settings

In the theme editor of your Salla dashboard (customize theme). Setting names in the editor are in Arabic only; the
English names in this guide are translations.

- **General theme settings**: in the settings list. Ruwa's own settings sit under headings starting with "رُوى:"
  (Ruwa). See [Theme settings](./settings).
- **Home page blocks**: add, order and hide them in the blocks list. Ruwa's blocks start with "Ruwa:". See
  [Home page blocks](./home-blocks).
- **Logo, store colour, font, menus**: from your Salla store settings; the theme follows them.

## First steps

1. Pick a **brand colour**, or leave it empty to follow your Salla store colour.
2. If your logo is dark, add a **dark-mode logo** so it stays clear on the dark background.
3. Choose the **default mode** (light, dark, or the visitor's device setting).
4. Build your home page from Ruwa's blocks. Start with the [suggested order](./home-blocks#a-suggested-home-page).
5. Replace each block's placeholder photos and text with your own.
6. Open your store in Arabic and English, in both modes, to check the result.
