# FAQ

## Why doesn't my store colour show?

If you filled in **Brand colour** in Zahiya's settings, it replaces the store colour. Clear it to follow your Salla
store colour.

## I changed "Dark mode" in the footer section and nothing changed

Zahiya's footer always uses your brand colour; that setting is for the base theme's footer.

## Why is there no email field for a newsletter?

Salla doesn't offer newsletter sign-up to themes. The "Join us" block and the footer's join column are buttons: they
follow your link, or open the sign-up window for guests.

## The countdown in "Deal product with countdown" doesn't show

Write the end date as `2026-12-31 23:59`, or leave it empty and give the product a discount with an end date in
Salla.

## My Arabic text shows on English pages

Write the English version in the editor's English field. See [Languages and dark mode](./languages).

## The cart drawer doesn't open on phones

That's intended: on phones, the cart tab in the bottom bar opens the cart page.

## The offer popup doesn't show a second time

It shows once per visit (browser session). Open the store in a private window to see it again.
