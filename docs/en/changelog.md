# Changelog

## Version 1.0 (coming soon)

The first release of Zahiya on Salla's Twilight React engine:

- 19 home page blocks of its own, including a video hero, before and after, a shoppable lookbook and reels, and a
  deal with a countdown.
- Light and dark mode, with a logo per mode.
- Arabic and English on every page.
- A floating header, a scrolling top bar and a welcome offer popup.
- A cart drawer on computers and tablets.
- A product page with tabs, an Ask a question button and a sticky add button on phones.
- A footer in your brand colour with your store's name in large letters.
